Anomaly detection experiment: autoencoder-based approach with per-category training, 
bottleneck compression, and threshold analysis - documented limitations and comparison with supervised approach


"I tried four approaches for product generalization. The autoencoder achieved 29% 
defect recall - insufficient because reconstruction errors between good and defective 
parts overlapped too much. The fundamental issue is that a basic convolutional 
autoencoder learns texture reconstruction, not semantic 'goodness.'

For production, I'd use PatchCore or EfficientAD which extract patch-level 
features from pretrained networks and use nearest-neighbor scoring - these achieve 
99% AUROC on MVTec AD. My per-category ResNet18 classifier at 89% accuracy is 
the current working solution, with the understanding that each new product line needs its own training run."
